Outside Projects

I specialize in the fields of natural resource economics, environmental economics, and agricultural economics, including environmental markets and finance, water rights and markets, groundwater rights and management, fishery rights and management, agricultural land markets, and crop production and governmental crop programs.

Services include economic feasibility studies, economic impact assessments, environmental and agricultural impact statements, and valuations related to agriculture and water.

Example Activities

  • Economic impact analysis of North Carolina's commercial fishing industry
  • Economic impact analysis of shellfish mariculture in NC
  • Agricultural impact analysis of hemp and hemp processing in eastern NC
  • Proposal preparation for reduced carbon agricultural practices
  • Economic feasibility analysis of novel agricultural water management practices
  • Preparation of outreach materials and presentations
